Policy Timeline

Every Swedish and Japanese energy and climate policy decision in the dataset, both countries on one time axis and one pixel scale so the two are directly comparable. Sweden (SE) is the upper lane, Japan (JP) the lower. A marker is a decision; the bar behind it is how long that instrument stayed in force; the band under each lane is who was in government at the time. Marker shape carries legal status and marker colour carries direction of travel, so neither is read from colour alone. Nothing on this page is typed by hand: it is rendered from sources/policy/policy_history_dataset.md by scripts/build_policy_timeline.py.
